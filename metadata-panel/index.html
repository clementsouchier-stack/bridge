What is ID3?

ID3 is a metadata standard that stores information directly inside audio files, especially MP3s. It can contain the title, artist, album, track number, genre, BPM, key, credits and more.

In this panel: a subtle dot next to a label means it has an ID3 equivalent. Not every Bridge field is an ID3 field.

ID3 compatibility does not necessarily mean Bridge currently imports or exports every mapped field. Multiple releases are managed in Bridge, not by one ID3 album value.
