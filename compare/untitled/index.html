Frequently asked

Before you make the choice.

Is Bridge.audio a direct replacement for [untitled]?

Not for every use case. [untitled] is especially strong for editing ideas and collaborating on demos, including version history and offline work. Bridge focuses on organized music libraries, professional sharing and discovery. Many artists may use both at different stages.

Can I work with unfinished music on Bridge?

Yes. You can upload recordings and demos, organize them in projects, enrich their metadata and share them privately. But Bridge does not currently provide [untitled]'s full version history or equivalent in-app recording and editing tools.

Does Bridge have AI music search?

Yes. Talent Pro and other eligible Pro plans include advanced auto-tagging and Smart Search, including Prompt Search and Similarity Search. Bridge Free currently offers basic auto-tagging, not advanced Smart Search.

Can Bridge help my music get discovered by industry professionals?

Eligible music can be published to Bridge Sync for discovery by audiovisual professionals, subject to selection, plan and publication conditions. Not every artist or track is automatically searchable by every professional.

Is Bridge Talents available now?

No. Bridge Talents is a publicly announced Discovery Hub under development. It is planned to focus on broader artist discoverability, but it is not currently available. This page compares features you can use today.

Which is better for artists?

Choose [untitled] if your priority is recording ideas, handling versions and sharing unfinished tracks in a mobile-first workflow. Choose Bridge when you need searchable catalogs, music metadata, team collaboration and professional presentation and discoverability. The tools can be complementary.
